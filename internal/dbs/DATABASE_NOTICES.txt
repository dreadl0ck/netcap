dbip-city-lite.mmdb, dbip-asn-lite.mmdb (layout 2 only)
  IP Geolocation by DB-IP: https://db-ip.com/
  Copyright DB-IP / Eris Networks S.A.S. Data is licensed under Creative
  Commons Attribution 4.0 International:
  https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/
  DB-IP incorporates GeoNames data: https://www.geonames.org/
  GeoNames attribution license: https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/
  Source: https://db-ip.com/db/lite.php
  Release month, download URL and SHA-256 are recorded in geoip-sources.json.
  MMDB data is unmodified; gzip compression is removed and filenames are
  normalized. Country/city estimates are approximate. The free Lite editions
  have reduced accuracy and coverage and are updated monthly.
  Web applications using this data must link to DB-IP on result pages.

OPTIONAL USER-INSTALLED DATA (not included in this archive)
  Nmap probes: review https://nmap.org/npsl/ and obtain nmap-service-probes
  from your Nmap installation or https://svn.nmap.org/nmap/nmap-service-probes.
  Copy it into your Netcap database directory under that exact filename.
  NPSL section 3 addresses programs reading this file; a separate download
  does not resolve the reader's licensing requirements.
  MaxMind GeoLite2: create an account at https://www.maxmind.com/ and accept
  its terms. Download and extract GeoLite2-ASN.mmdb and GeoLite2-City.mmdb
  into your Netcap database directory. Keep these user-local downloads out
  of redistributed archives. Instructions:
  https://github.com/dreadl0ck/netcap/blob/master/internal/dbs/README.md#optional-user-installed-databases
  The retired Alexa domain whitelist is no longer distributed.
