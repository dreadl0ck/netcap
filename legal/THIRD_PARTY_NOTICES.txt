Corresponding source for Netcap
-------------------------------
The complete corresponding source for a release is available at no charge from
the matching tag at:

  https://github.com/dreadl0ck/netcap/releases
  https://github.com/dreadl0ck/netcap/tags

The release tag and `net --version` identify the distributed version. Build and
installation scripts are included in that source tree.
